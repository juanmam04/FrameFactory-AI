# Research — The CEO Who Bet It All on a Sandwich

## Topic
In 1985, David Chang was just a young chef trying to make a name for himself in the culinary world. After years of struggling and working in various restaurants, he decided to open his own establishment in New York City, focusing solely on one menu item: the pork bun. His obsession with creating the perfect version led to the birth of Momofuku, which would become a global culinary empire. But the road to success was filled with challenges, fierce competition, and the pressure of high expectations.
