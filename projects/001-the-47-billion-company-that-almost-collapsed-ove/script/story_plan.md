# Story Plan

## Central story
In September 2019, WeWork's planned IPO was abruptly pulled, leading to a valuation drop from $47 billion to just $8 billion in a matter of weeks.

## Central question
How did WeWork go from a $47 billion valuation to a mere $8 billion in a matter of weeks?

## Core contradiction
The promise of a revolutionary coworking model clashed with unsustainable business practices and leadership issues.

## Stakes
The fate of a company that was once seen as a leader in the coworking space hung in the balance, with billions of dollars and thousands of jobs at stake.

## Hook
In September 2019, WeWork's planned IPO was abruptly pulled, leading to a valuation drop from $47 billion to just $8 billion in a matter of weeks.

## Ending state
WeWork goes public via a SPAC merger in 2021, valuing the company at approximately $9 billion.

## Characters
- **Adam Neumann** — Co-founder and CEO | To lead WeWork to unprecedented heights and secure its place in the market. | Oversaw rapid expansion and raised significant capital, but faced backlash for governance issues.
- **Miguel McKelvey** — Co-founder | To create innovative coworking spaces and support the company's growth. | Worked alongside Neumann to expand WeWork's global presence.
- **SoftBank** — Major investor | To capitalize on the booming coworking market and support WeWork's growth. | Invested billions into WeWork, significantly increasing its valuation.
