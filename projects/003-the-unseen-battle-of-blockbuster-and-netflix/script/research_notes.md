## Timeline

- **1985**: Blockbuster is founded in Dallas, Texas. It becomes the largest video rental chain in the world.
- **1997**: Netflix is founded by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph, starting as a DVD rental service with a pay-per-rental model.
- **1999**: Netflix launches its subscription rental service, eliminating late fees and setting the stage for future growth.
- **2000**: Reed Hastings offers to sell Netflix to Blockbuster for $50 million, but Blockbuster dismisses the offer.
- **2002**: Netflix goes public with an IPO, raising $82.5 million.
- **2004**: Netflix surpasses 1.5 million subscribers, highlighting a shift in consumer preferences.
- **2005**: Blockbuster launches its own online rental service but struggles to compete with Netflix.
- **2007**: Netflix introduces streaming services, further disrupting the traditional rental model.
- **2010**: Blockbuster files for bankruptcy, marking the end of an era in video rental.
- **2013**: Netflix wins its first Emmy Award for 'House of Cards,' transitioning into content creation.
- **2015**: Netflix reaches 70 million subscribers globally, solidifying its leadership in the entertainment industry.
- **2020**: Netflix surpasses 200 million subscribers, reflecting its dominance in the streaming market.
