# Documentary editorial definition (source of truth)

Canonical code: `src/documentary/editorial.py` (+ `story_architect.py`)

## What we make

**Fascinating true stories about companies.**

- Story first. Business second.
- Nonfiction only (no invented narrator/scenes/dialogue).
- Not MBA education, corporate analysis, advice listicles, Reddit fiction.

## World-class craft pipeline

1. **Research (AI)** — harvest story ammunition (desire, pressure, ironies, cold opens, specifics)
2. **Story architecture** — engine, cold open, beat sheet, withholds, ending image
3. **Draft** — narration following architecture + craft bible
4. **Polish** — rhythm/curiosity only; no new facts

## Viewer test

After watching, they should think: *“I had no idea that company had that story.”*  
And: *“I need to know what happens next.”*

Not: *“I sat through a business lesson.”*
